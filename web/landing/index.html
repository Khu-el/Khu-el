NTE Web Apps

Four planning tools: scenario calculators, diligence checklists, and draft decision memos. Sign in separately to each — your data syncs to your own account, shared only where noted below.

Planning tools

  • 🏠 Deal Architect
    Real estate deal intelligence: wholesaling, seller-finance, and hold analysis.
  • 📊 Capital Readiness
    Entity, cap table, and offering-readiness diligence — not an investor-solicitation tool.
  • 📄 Notes Underwriting
    Distressed-debt analysis: valuation, scenarios, and lien diligence — analysis only.
  • 🗂️ Legacy & Estate Coordination
    Shared family workspace — CCRLT, House of Ransom, and related family records.

Elsewhere on the network

  • 🏛️ Neterverse Administration Trust DAOSource only
    Public-facing portal for the trust. Static page, not yet hosted on this domain.
  • 🧠 The Mental Performance PlaybookLocal only
    90-day sprint and daily discipline tracker. Runs in your browser; nothing is hosted or synced. Deliberately not published here.

How this network is wired

  • 🌐 Domain property map
    Which hostname serves what, the DNS rows still to be added, and what is already verified.
  • 🔌 Connector and plugin registry
    Every available integration, which property it serves, and what it is not allowed to do.
  • 🎛️ Executive OS standard
    The evidence, visual, and approval rules every property on this network follows.

These are planning tools. They produce scenarios, not guarantees, and drafts, not filings. Nothing here is legal, tax, or investment advice.
