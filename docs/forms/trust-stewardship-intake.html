Property information

What exists, how it is titled, and who is named on it. Titling and beneficiary designations override a will or a trust — collect them as facts, not estimates.

Two columns that matter mostFor every asset, the two columns people skip are exact name on title and current named beneficiary. A plan that ignores them is a plan that will be contradicted at the worst possible moment.

05

Existing records

What documents the family believes exist, and what can actually be produced. Those are different questions and both get asked.

The rule that governs this sheetA document is not verified because it is named, dated, or found in a folder. Until a signed copy is in hand and its signature page has been looked at, the entry stays believed to exist. Blank execution lines are common and are a finding, not a formality.
Ask for a deed, an assignment, or a retitled account statement. Signing a trust and funding a trust are separate acts, and the second one is skipped constantly.
Move to another state, marriage, divorce, death, birth, business sold or started, property bought or sold, a named person who is no longer suitable.
