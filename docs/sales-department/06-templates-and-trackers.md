# VI — Templates and Trackers · items 41–48

🔵 PROPOSED method draft · Claude Code · 2026-10-25. Rules in [README.md](README.md) apply to every item.
Everything in `[BRACKETS]` is a placeholder. **A filled-in version of any template is a record and
does not belong in this repository** — it belongs in the capacity's system of record.
Every message template is a **draft for the principal's approval; none is sent by the assistant.**

---

## 41 · Initial DM — *one question, no pitch*
```
Subject/opener: [one verifiable, sourced observation about their situation]
Body: [Why that caught my attention, one sentence.] [One genuine question about how they handle [problem].]
Close: [No ask beyond an answer. Easy to ignore.]
Source for the observation (not sent): [URL, date]
```
**Check before approving:** observation is true and sourced · ≤ 4 sentences · no pitch · channel is permitted.

## 42 · Meeting Booking DM — *the three-minute video offer*
```
[Reference to their reply or signal, one sentence.]
[Offer: a [3]-minute video on [their specific problem], or [15] minutes live — their choice.]
[Two concrete time options in their time zone, or a booking link the principal owns.]
```
**Rule:** the offer is something the principal will actually deliver. A video offer means the video exists to be made.

## 43 · Backup DM — *the nudge built on one detail*
```
[A new, specific, useful detail since the last message — not "bumping this".]
[One sentence connecting it to their problem.]
[A clean, optional next step — or permission to close the loop.]
```

## 44 · Follow-Up Sequence — *day 3, day 7, day 14*
| Touch | Timing | Content rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | day 3 | one new useful fact; one question |
| 2 | day 7 | a different angle or a relevant public resource |
| 3 | day 14 | a polite close: "[I'll step back — reply if useful later]" |

**Stop rules:** a reply (any) pauses the sequence and routes to the principal · a "no" or opt-out
ends it permanently and is recorded · no touch beyond day 14 without a new signal. Timing is the principal's to change.

## 45 · Call Notes Template — *the debrief in five lines*
```
1. Who/when: [account · attendees (roles) · date]
2. Problem in their words: ["…"]
3. Impact / cost they named: [their units, or UNKNOWN]
4. Decision process & timeline: [stated · or UNKNOWN]
5. Agreed next step: [owner · date]  |  Objections: [list]  |  Inferences (not stated): [list]
```

## 48 · Four-Week Rollout — *week one to compounding*
| Week | Focus | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ICP v1 (01), first 25 accounts with evidence (02–05), trackers set up (46–47) | the principal approves the ICP and watch-list |
| 2 | Fit-score and rank (10, 07); first approved batch of drafts (12, 41) | the principal has approved and sent the batch themselves |
| 3 | Replies, discovery (13, 45), follow-ups (44); first weekly report (16) | each reply has a recorded next step |
| 4 | Retrospective: what worked, ICP corrections, objections (26); update the sales memory (21) | ICP v2 written; the next four weeks planned |

**Rule:** "compounding" is a hypothesis to measure with real counts at week 4, not a promise.
