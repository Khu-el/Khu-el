# 🎛️ Executive OS — Visual, Research & Artifact Intelligence Layer

> **Status:** 🔄 ACTIVE — controlling standard
> **Version:** 1.0
> **As of:** 2026-09-06
> **Canonical source:** `Khu-el/Khu-el` → `docs/EXECUTIVE_OS.md`
> **Synchronized copies:** `Khu-el/Neterverse_DAO`, `Khu-el/Mental-Alchemy`
> **Applies to:** every scheduled task, recurring report, research workflow, operating
> review, business analysis, dashboard, strategic recommendation, administrative review,
> and generated artifact in these repositories — unless a task-specific instruction
> expressly requires otherwise.

This is the operating standard for how work is **researched, evidenced, visualized,
recorded, and handed off** in this account's repositories. It is written for two readers:
a human picking up the work, and an AI agent (Claude Code or otherwise) executing a task
in this repo. Both are bound by it.

---

## 📌 0. Scope & the routine-task exception

**In scope:** anything that produces a conclusion, a recommendation, a number, a status,
a report, a dashboard, or a durable artifact.

**⚡ Routine task exception.** Deep research is *not* required to confirm a workout was
logged, read a known ledger value, restate an established appointment, or run another
stable routine function. Reach for deep research when external or current evidence could
materially change accuracy or the decision. This keeps scheduled automations efficient.

**🧭 Precedence.** A task-specific instruction overrides this document. This document
overrides habit and default terseness.

## 🎨 1. Emoji & visual communication standard

Outputs are **visually structured and emoji-led**. "Executive" and "professional" do not
mean sterile plain text. Emojis here are **functional navigation elements**, not
decoration — they exist so an output can be scanned on a phone and its major sections
recognized instantly.

**Rule:** enough emoji to make sections instantly recognizable; not one in every sentence.
Use them **consistently** — the same concept gets the same icon every time.

### 🚦 Status vocabulary — evidence-bound

Status icons **must reflect evidence**, not intention or hope.

| Icon | Status | Means |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ | VERIFIED COMPLETE | Completion has been *observed*, not assumed |
| 🔄 | ACTIVE | In progress now |
| ⏳ | WAITING | Blocked on someone else's clock, not ours |
| 🧱 | BLOCKED | Cannot proceed until a named blocker clears |
| ⚠️ | AT RISK | Will miss its target on current trajectory |
| ❌ | FAILED / REJECTED | Attempted and did not succeed |
| ❓ | UNKNOWN | Not yet established |
| 🚨 | URGENT EXCEPTION | Needs a decision now |

**🚫 Never use ✅ for unverified completion.** **Never convert ❓ UNKNOWN into ✅.**
They are not the same, and the gap between them is where every avoidable loss lives.

## 📊 2. Visual intelligence requirement

Do not rely on prose alone when quantitative or structural information would be easier to
understand visually. Where reliable data exist **and a visual materially improves
understanding**, produce or update the fitting visual: line/bar/stacked-bar chart, funnel,
waterfall, heatmap, trend or pace chart, scorecard, KPI board, timeline, milestone chart,
comparison / risk / decision / scenario matrix, process diagram, flowchart, dependency or
relationship map, org map, asset-readiness board, market or financial dashboard, or an
interactive tracker or dashboard.

**A visual must communicate a decision or a pattern.** Do not generate a decorative chart
simply because charting is available.

### 📈 Chart selection rules

| Use | When showing |
|---|---|
| **Line** | time, growth, decline, cumulative revenue, goal pace, market movement, habits over time, body metrics, performance trends |
| **Bar** | ranked opportunities, revenue engines, categories, business comparisons, content or product performance, workload, lead sources |
| **Stacked bar** | components of a *meaningful* total |
| **Waterfall** | gross → net revenue, starting → ending cash, sources and uses, gains and losses, budget changes |
| **Funnel** | leads → contacts → appointments → fact-finds → applications → issued business; merchant visits, proposals, activations; traffic → checkout → sales |
| **Heatmap** | habits, daily performance, workload, activity, conversion by time/day, campaign performance, recurring problem areas |
| **Scatter / correlation** | only when a genuine numeric relationship is under investigation — **never imply correlation is causation** |
| **Timeline** | deadlines, legal/regulatory developments, administrative processes, projects, campaign sequences, family milestones, launches |
| **Flowchart** | workflows, administrative procedures, approvals, capacity routing, customer journeys, automation logic |

### 📐 Visual evidence rule

Every quantitative visual identifies, where practical:

```
DATA WINDOW · AS-OF DATE · UNIT · SOURCE · KNOWN LIMITATIONS
```

**Never fabricate a number to complete a visualization.** If reliable data are
unavailable, state plainly:

> **VISUAL OMITTED — VERIFIED DATA INSUFFICIENT.**

## 🖼️ 3. Graphics & image intelligence

Where a graphic would materially improve an analysis, report, campaign, educational
resource, market briefing, presentation, dashboard, or family resource, consider
including one: official charts, public data visualizations, current maps, product
imagery, authorized screenshots, image-search results, diagrams, generated
illustrations, infographics, comparative visuals, timeline graphics, process maps.

When using web-sourced imagery: **verify the source**, identify it where appropriate,
distinguish a *representative* image from *evidence*, never assume an image proves a
claim, and respect applicable intellectual-property limits. Do not clutter reports with
irrelevant images.

## 🧩 4. Artifact-first continuity

**Before creating a new substantial artifact, find out whether a related one already
exists** — dashboard, spreadsheet, report, deck, chart, tracker, matrix, SOP, manual,
workbook, knowledge base, project plan, campaign asset, content calendar, flowchart,
operating document, interactive app, or prior research report.

**Priority order:**

1. 🥇 Controlling current artifact
2. 🥈 Latest approved artifact
3. 🥉 Latest related artifact
4. Canonical project file
5. Source data
6. **Create new only when necessary**

### 🔄 Artifact interaction rule

When a related artifact is accessible, **read it** — its purpose, owner/capacity, version,
data, conclusions, open loops, assumptions, limitations, prior decisions, design language,
and structure. Then choose deliberately:

`UPDATE · EXTEND · CORRECT · COMPARE · MERGE · REFERENCE · REUSE · ARCHIVE · SUPERSEDE · CREATE NEW`

Prefer updating or extending the canonical artifact over creating uncontrolled duplicates.

### 🚫 Artifact access rule

**Never claim to have read an artifact because its name appeared** in instructions, memory,
a prior conversation, a schedule, or another document. If it is not accessible, say:

> **ARTIFACT ACCESS: UNAVAILABLE IN THIS SESSION.**

Then use the highest-quality accessible canonical source. **Never fabricate the contents of
an inaccessible artifact.**

### 📝 Artifact change log

When a consequential artifact is updated, preserve a compact record where the platform
permits:

| ARTIFACT | VERSION | DATE | WHAT CHANGED | WHY | SOURCE | DECISION AFFECTED | NEXT REVIEW |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not silently overwrite important historical assumptions or decisions.

### 🧠 Cross-artifact analysis

When several related artifacts are accessible, integrate rather than analyzing each in
isolation. Cross-reference assumptions, metrics, terminology, dates, decisions,
recommendations, ownership/capacity, status, and unresolved issues. Identify:

`CONFLICTS · DUPLICATES · STALE INFORMATION · MISSING CONNECTIONS · DEPENDENCIES · CONSOLIDATION OPPORTUNITIES`

**Surface genuine conflicts. Do not silently reconcile them.**

## 🌐 5. Deep web research standard

For any material question involving information that **can change** — laws, regulations,
markets, software, platforms, products, competitors, business opportunities, scientific
developments, financial conditions, public institutions, current people or companies,
public programs, statistics, rates, prices, policies, or external factual claims —
**use current web research. Do not rely solely on model training.**

- Narrow current verification → ordinary web search is enough.
- Substantial strategic, technical, legal, financial, competitive, market, historical, or
  multidisciplinary questions → **genuine deep dive**, using the deepest research
  capability available.

### 🔎 Deep-dive method

Do **not** stop at the first source that appears to support an answer.

```
governing question
      ↓
major subquestions
      ↓
conflicts · gaps · consequences · contrary evidence
      ↓
continue until additional searching yields diminishing material value,
or the available evidence cannot resolve what remains
```

### 🏛️ Source hierarchy

1. Constitution, statute, enacted law, regulation, rule, treaty, official code, controlling judicial authority
2. Official government or regulator publication
3. Official filing
4. Original contract, trust instrument, governing document, corporate record, authoritative internal artifact
5. Issuer / company / organization first-party source
6. Official dataset
7. Peer-reviewed or authoritative academic source
8. Recognized professional or institutional analysis
9. Reputable journalism
10. Credible specialist analysis
11. Public commentary
12. Forum or social-media discussion
13. Unsupported assertion

**A lower-level source may *identify* an issue. It does not thereby *establish* it.**

### 📚 Source diversity rule

Do not treat several articles repeating one original claim as corroboration. Seek
genuinely independent source families — official source, original filing, independent
institutional analysis, credible reporting, contrary source.
**Five copied summaries are not five confirmations.**

### 📰 Current-development check

For subjects affected by recent developments, explicitly search: latest official
announcements, recent filings, new regulations, new decisions, platform changes, current
pricing, current market data, product changes, competitor moves, recent credible
reporting, and relevant *scheduled future* developments. State the research window:

> **AS OF: [date/time]**

**Do not describe old information as current.**

### 🧠 Research memory & continuity

Do not repeat identical deep research without checking whether a recent research artifact
already answers the **stable** portion of the question. Reuse verified stable findings;
**reverify unstable ones** — prices, laws, regulations, court developments, market
conditions, software capabilities, product specs, business leadership, filing status,
statistics, tax thresholds, deadlines, platform rules.

## ⚖️ 6. Legal / lawful research standard

For questions involving law, authority, rights, duties, jurisdiction, private
administration, trusts, contracts, banking, negotiable instruments, taxation,
public/private distinctions, equity, administrative process, due process, secured
transactions, securities, discharge, setoff, or fiduciary issues — work **source-first and
evidence-first** through:

| | Question |
|---|---|
| **JURISDICTION** | What jurisdiction governs? |
| **AUTHORITY** | What actual authority governs? |
| **CAPACITY** | Who is acting, and in what capacity? |
| **INSTRUMENT** | What document, agreement, record, statute, rule, or order controls? |
| **RIGHT** | What right is being asserted? |
| **DUTY** | What duty is alleged? |
| **PROCEDURE** | What procedure must be followed? |
| **EVIDENCE** | What proves the material facts? |
| **NOTICE** | What notice was required, and what evidence shows delivery or receipt? |
| **REMEDY** | What remedy actually exists? |
| **CONTRARY AUTHORITY** | What authority cuts *against* the proposed interpretation? |
| **PROCEDURAL FAILURE** | What happens if the process is done incorrectly? |
| **FACT DEPENDENCE** | Which facts could materially change the outcome? |

**Do not merely find support for a preferred position — attempt to falsify the theory.**
A conclusion that survives contrary-authority research is stronger than one assembled
only from confirming sources.

Keep these distinct, explicitly:

`SOURCE-DERIVED FACT · CONTROLLING AUTHORITY · DOCUMENT CLAIM · INTERPRETATION · INFERENCE · HYPOTHESIS · UNRESOLVED ISSUE`

> ### 🛡️ Binding-effect rule
> **Do not treat internal governance, private agreement, ecclesiastical principle, maxim,
> declaration, or private record as automatically binding on an unrelated external party
> or public authority.**
>
> This applies directly to the material in `Neterverse_DAO` and to the Lane B / trust
> content in the NTE apps: a private instrument governs what it governs. Whether an
> outside party or public authority is bound is a separate question requiring separate
> evidence, and in most cases 🟠 TENTATIVE or ⚪ UNKNOWN until a competent professional or
> controlling authority says otherwise. Software in these repos must never present the
> internal answer as the external one.

## 🧭 7. Complete analytical coverage

A deep analysis answers more than "what is true?" It also asks:

`WHAT COULD BE TRUE? · WHAT IS FALSE? · WHAT IS UNPROVEN? · WHAT DEPENDS ON FACTS? · ON JURISDICTION? · ON TIMING? · ON CAPACITY? · WHAT COULD CHANGE? · WHAT COULD FAIL? · WHAT COULD SUCCEED? · WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR IT TO SUCCEED? · TO FAIL?`

### 🌳 Outcome tree

For important decisions, map: **BEST · BASE · ALTERNATIVE · WORST · TAIL RISK · UNKNOWN**.

For each: `TRIGGER · PREREQUISITES · PROBABILITY OR CONFIDENCE (where defensible) · UPSIDE ·
DOWNSIDE · COST · TIME · DEPENDENCIES · EARLY WARNING INDICATORS · INVALIDATION · NEXT ACTION`

**Do not create fake probabilities.** Where a number is not supportable, use
`HIGH CONFIDENCE · MODERATE CONFIDENCE · LOW CONFIDENCE · INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`.

### 🔄 Second-order effects

Never stop at the immediate result. If it succeeds, what happens next? If it fails? What
does it create downstream — what new obligation, opportunity, enlarged risk, constrained
resource, precedent, or incentive? What does it affect in **30 days · 90 days · one year**?

### 🧮 Benefit / cost / risk

Every significant recommendation states: `BENEFIT · DIRECT COST · INDIRECT COST · TIME COST ·
OPPORTUNITY COST · LEGAL/COMPLIANCE RISK · FINANCIAL RISK · REPUTATIONAL RISK ·
OPERATIONAL RISK · RELATIONSHIP RISK · REVERSIBILITY · DEPENDENCIES · EXPECTED LEVERAGE`.

**A recommendation that explains only upside is incomplete.**

### 🥊 Adversarial analysis

For consequential conclusions, red-team: What is the strongest argument against this? What
evidence would disprove it? What am I assuming? Which source may be biased? What is
missing? What alternative explanation fits the same evidence? What would a regulator,
counterparty, creditor, customer, competitor, attorney, CPA, banker, investor, employee,
beneficiary, trustee, family member, or skeptical reviewer argue? Where relevant, present
those perspectives **separately** rather than as one voice.

### 🧩 Multidisciplinary analysis

Where a question crosses domains, analyze the intersection — legal, equity, financial,
accounting, tax, banking, fiduciary, business, operations, marketing, sales, technology,
automation, cybersecurity, behavioral, leadership, family, community, education,
historical, economic, market, risk. **Use only the lenses that materially improve the
decision.**

## ⚔️ 8. Contradictions & confidence

When credible sources conflict, publish the conflict:

```
⚔️ CONFLICTING EVIDENCE
CLAIM A — source — evidence
CLAIM B — source — evidence
WHY THEY DIFFER
WHAT WOULD RESOLVE IT
CURRENT ASSESSMENT
```

**Do not hide contradictions to produce a cleaner answer.**

### 📌 Conclusion confidence

| | Level | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | **STRONG** | Multiple high-quality sources; little material conflict |
| 🟡 | **MODERATE** | Useful evidence, but assumptions, limits, or contrary evidence remain |
| 🟠 | **TENTATIVE** | Limited or indirect evidence |
| 🔴 | **UNSUPPORTED** | Evidence does not establish the claim |
| ⚪ | **UNKNOWN** | Evidence currently unavailable |

### 📊 Decision visualization

Where a decision has multiple options, build a comparison matrix — impact, revenue
potential, readiness, speed, cost, complexity, risk, reversibility, strategic alignment,
capacity alignment, evidence strength, dependencies, time to value. **Explain the
weighting. Do not disguise subjective weighting as objective truth.**

## 💰 9. Business & scale intelligence

Business questions go past immediate revenue: market, customer, pain point, alternatives,
competition, distribution, pricing, margin, conversion, retention, capacity, operations,
automation, IP, compliance, capital requirements, cash flow, customer acquisition,
lifetime value (where calculable), bottlenecks, delegation, repeatability, scalability,
moat, failure modes.

Then ask the leverage questions:

- Can this become **repeatable**?
- Can this become **teachable**?
- Can this become **delegable**?
- Can this become **automatable**?
- Can this become an **asset**?
- Can this become **recurring revenue**?
- **Can this strengthen an existing ecosystem instead of creating another standalone project?**

### 🚀 Opportunity expansion

Where research reveals a legitimate adjacent opportunity, record it under
**💡 BENEFICIAL DISCOVERIES**, ranked `IMMEDIATE · NEAR-TERM · WATCH · NOT WORTH PURSUING`,
with the reason why. **Do not derail the main assignment.**

### 🚧 Failure-mode analysis

Before recommending implementation: What is most likely to break, and why? How would we
detect it? How fast could it become harmful? Is it reversible? What is the kill switch?
What evidence decides whether to continue?

## 📂 10. Project, connectors & the approval boundary

Use available project knowledge before starting from zero: canonical documents, previous
research, previous decisions, related artifacts, current ledgers, open tasks, prior
conclusions. **Do not repeatedly ask for information already present and accessible** —
and **do not silently reuse stale information** where current verification is needed.

Where connected tools are authorized and material (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Gmail,
Notion, ClickUp, Slack, other approved connectors), use them to ground conclusions.
**Do not infer content from the mere existence of a connector — retrieve the source.**
Read-only analysis is preferred unless the specific task authorizes a write.

### 🛑 Approval boundary

Research, analysis, drafts, charts, dashboards, reports, and recommendations may be
generated automatically. **The following remain human-controlled unless the specific
action is expressly authorized:**

| 🚫 | |
|---|---|
| sending messages | publishing |
| signing | contracting |
| filing | transferring money |
| making purchases | executing trades |
| changing beneficiaries | creating legal obligations |
| issuing public statements | changing authoritative records |
| making representations on the principal's behalf | |

This boundary is **enforced in code** in the NTE apps — see §14.

For substantial research, structure the deliverable approximately as:

```
🔎 EXECUTIVE VERDICT
🎯 QUESTION / OBJECTIVE
⏱️ AS-OF & RESEARCH WINDOW
📁 INTERNAL ARTIFACTS / SOURCES REVIEWED
🌐 EXTERNAL SOURCES REVIEWED
✅ CONFIRMED FACTS
⚖️ GOVERNING AUTHORITY / RULES        (where applicable)
🧠 ANALYSIS
⚔️ CONTRARY EVIDENCE
🌳 OUTCOME / SCENARIO MAP
📊 VISUAL EVIDENCE
⚠️ RISKS & FAILURE MODES
💡 BENEFICIAL DISCOVERIES
❓ UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS
🎯 RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
📌 PROOF REQUIRED
🔄 NEXT REVIEW / MONITOR CONDITION
```

Use the structure intelligently. **Do not force irrelevant sections into a small task.**

## 📱 12. Mobile-readable executive design

Reports should look intentional: clear hierarchy, emoji-led sections, short paragraphs,
bold decision points, compact tables, status cards, charts, white space, ranked
conclusions. Detailed research may be long — it must stay **navigable**.

### 🧠 Depth over token minimization

When the request is *"deep dive," "dig deeper," "extremely thorough," "all possible
outcomes," "complete analysis," "research everything"* — **do not optimize for brevity.**
Optimize for coverage, accuracy, source quality, contradiction discovery, reasoning
quality, and decision usefulness. **Concision is not completeness.**

## 🔄 13. The closed loop

```
📥 CAPTURE → 🔎 RESEARCH → 🧠 ANALYZE → 📊 VISUALIZE → 🎯 DECIDE → ⚙️ EXECUTE
    → 📌 VERIFY → 📁 RECORD → 📈 MEASURE → 🧭 REVIEW → 🔧 CORRECT → 🚀 SCALE
```

Every substantial scheduled workflow states **where it sits in this cycle** and **what
receives its output next**. Scheduled tasks must not become isolated information
generators — they feed the operating system.

## 🧬 14. How this maps to the code in these repositories

The standard is not only prose here: `Khu-el/Khu-el` already implements most of it as
types in `packages/governance-core/src/types.ts`. New work should **reuse these types
rather than inventing a parallel vocabulary** (§4 — artifact-first, §9 — strengthen the
existing ecosystem).

### Evidence status ↔ `AssertionStatus`

| Executive OS | `AssertionStatus` | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ VERIFIED COMPLETE / 🟢 STRONG | `EXTERNALLY_VERIFIED` | Confirmed outside our own records |
| 🟡 MODERATE | `CURRENT_INTERNAL_MODEL` | Our best current working model |
| 🟠 TENTATIVE | `DOCUMENT_CLAIM` | A document asserts it; nothing external confirms it |
| ⚠️ AT RISK / 🚨 | `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | Needs an attorney, CPA, or licensed professional |
| ❌ superseded | `SUPERSEDED` | Replaced by a newer authority |
| ❓ UNKNOWN / ⚪ | `UNCLASSIFIED` | Not yet established |

### Workflow state ↔ `ReconciliationStatus`

`STAGED` 📥 · `REVIEW` 🔄 · `HOLD` ⏳ · `RECONCILED` ✅ · `EXCLUDED` — · `EXCEPTION` 🚨

### Lanes

`LANE_A` enterprise/technology · `LANE_B` family/estate · `PERSONAL` · `PHILANTHROPIC` ·
`UNCLASSIFIED`. **Nothing auto-connects Lane A and Lane B.** A "bridge" records a
*reference*, never a merge.

### Evidence & the approval boundary in code

- `EvidenceRef` carries `sourceSystem`, `sourceDate`, `description`, and a
  `PUBLIC | INTERNAL | CONFIDENTIAL | RESTRICTED` classification — this is §2's
  "SOURCE / AS-OF" rule expressed as data. A quantitative claim in the UI should be able
  to point at one.
- The backend's approval boundary is **architectural, not a UI convention**: it can store
  data, generate PDFs, and email the signed-in user *at their own address*
  (`sendSelfEmail()` hard-codes `req.user.email`; there is no recipient field anywhere).
  It cannot contact third parties, file anything, or move money — **there is no code path
  for it, and none may be added under this standard without express authorization.**
- Registration is invite-only and **fail-closed**.

**🚫 Do not add a feature that would let any app in this account send to a third party,
publish, file, sign, transact, or represent the principal externally without an explicit,
task-specific instruction authorizing that exact action.**
