ART. III

The ledger

Ranked by Performance Index — 65 % the quality of the engagement a post earned per thousand people reached, 35 % the size of the reach itself. A post that reaches few people but converts them hard still ranks; a post that reached everyone and moved no one does not.

# Post Reach Views Saves Shares Comments Follows EP / 1k Index
How the index is computed

Engagement points EP = likes×1 + comments×3 + shares×5 + saves×5 + follows×10. The weights are not vanity-weighted: a save and a share each cost a viewer something (filing it, or putting their name on it), a follow costs the most, a like costs nothing. EP / 1k divides that by reach. The index scales EP / 1k and reach against the best row in your own record, so it is always relative to your account, never to some benchmark that doesn't apply to a 1,280-follower niche audience.

ART. VI

The instrument itself

The four taxonomies the dossiers are scored against, written for this account's subject matter — private trust, equity, credit, and estate structure — not for generic creator advice.

Angle

The stance the video takes toward what the viewer already believes. It is not the topic. Two videos on the same trust clause with different angles perform an order of magnitude apart.

Hook — the first 3 to 5 seconds

What is said, out loud, before the viewer has decided anything. In this niche the audience arrives sceptical and slightly defensive; a hook that asks for patience gets none.

Openings that cost you the first three seconds
  • “Hey guys” / “What’s up family” — a greeting is a request for patience.
  • “In this video I’m going to explain” — announcing the explanation instead of starting it.
  • Any intro sting, logo, or slow push-in before a word is spoken.
  • Credentialing first (“I’ve been doing this 12 years”). Earn the right to say it at second 20, not second 1.
  • Starting on a caveat. Disclaimers belong in the caption, not the hook.

Title hook — the on-screen headline

The text card standing over the first frames. It is read before you are heard, and it is the entire pitch to everyone scrolling with sound off. It must not repeat the spoken hook — it should complete it, contradict it, or name the stake it leaves open.

Mechanical constraints
  • Six words maximum. Four is better.
  • Cap height at least one eighth of frame height — it must survive a 30 % thumbnail.
  • Upper third, but clear of Instagram's own chrome; keep roughly 15 % clear at top and 30 % at bottom.
  • Hold it for the full hook, not one second. If it leaves before the sentence lands, it never registered.
  • One weight, high contrast, no outline-plus-shadow-plus-glow. Legibility is the whole job.

Why it worked — the five checks

Run these against every post in Article III before you write a word in the why field. The answer is nearly always one of five, and naming which one is what makes the next video repeatable.
